Accolade Workshop

At a glance

  • 🟢 Version 0.1.1 · Targets CK3 1.21.0.3.
  • 🟢 Standalone mod; requires Tours & Tournaments.
  • 🟢 Create an accolade by choosing its attribute or its knight first.
  • 🟢 Languages: English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Spanish.
  • 🔴 Start a new campaign. Existing accolades have no migration for the rank-cap system.
  • 🔴 Changes accolade ranks and two interface windows; overlapping mods need a compatibility patch.

Choose the honour, then the knight

Looking for a particular accolade? Browse the attributes your court can provide, see how many candidates qualify, and choose the knight who will carry it. The familiar knight-first route remains available alongside it.

Two ways to create an accolade

The create button opens a choice of starting point:

  • Attribute first: browse available initial attributes with candidate and guest counts. Select an attribute to see the eligible knights and guests.
  • Knight first: use the familiar candidate list, then choose an eligible initial attribute.

Both routes finish in the normal creation panel. The game's eligibility checks, recruitment costs, knight controls and accolade creation costs still apply. Guests must be recruited before creating their accolade.

Set the highest rank

The Maximum accolade rank game rule offers 3, 6 (default), or 9, for players and AI. Glory is capped at the chosen rank's threshold: 600, 2,100, or 4,500 respectively.

Ranks 7–9 provide further attribute upgrades. Accolades still have at most three attributes, each with three levels; the game's Men-at-Arms attribute restriction remains.

Getting started

  1. Enable the mod and start a new campaign. Choose the maximum accolade rank in the game rules.
  2. Open the Military window, manage your knights and accolades, and click the accolade creation button.
  3. Choose your starting point, select a candidate and attribute, then confirm in the normal creation panel.

Compatibility and load order

Requires Tours & Tournaments; no other mod is required. Targets CK3 1.21.0.3.

Replaces window_create_accolade.gui and window_accolade.gui, changes accolade rank definitions, and adjusts the accolade rank-change notification hook. Mods changing those same elements need a merged compatibility patch; load order alone does not merge them. Total conversions and multiplayer compatibility are not established.

Saves and known limits

Use a new campaign. Existing accolades do not have the tracked Glory baseline required by the cap system. Migration and mid-campaign removal are not supported; keep a backup before changing a campaign's mod list.

Ranks 7–9 reuse the rank-6 badge ornament, with their own displayed rank names and numbers.
